Know the Red Flags

If you see any of these, stop the interaction and report it immediately.

Fake listings

Photos that look like stock images or prices that seem too good to be true.

Money requests

Asking for deposits, wire transfers, or gift cards before meeting.

Off-platform chat

Insisting on moving the conversation to WhatsApp, Telegram, or email.

Threats

Any form of aggressive language, intimidation, or harassment.

Scams

Offering "overpayment" by check or asking for your account credentials.

Document requests

Asking for copies of your ID, passport, or utility bills.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a suspicious listing? expand_more

Navigate to the item page and look for the 'Report' icon. Select the reason for reporting and our team will investigate within 24 hours.

Can I block a user? expand_more

Yes. From any chat or profile page, click 'Options' and select 'Block'. This will prevent them from seeing your listings or messaging you.

Someone is asking for money via bank transfer. expand_more

Never send money outside of the platform’s recommended payment methods or before seeing the item. Report these users immediately.

What happens after I file a report? expand_more

Our trust and safety team will review the evidence. If the user violated community guidelines, they may be warned or permanently banned.

What if I feel unsafe during a meeting? expand_more

Your safety is priority. Leave the area immediately to a secure public place and call local emergency services if necessary.
