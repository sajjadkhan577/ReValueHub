Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a suspicious user? expand_more
Simply click on the user's profile and tap the three dots in the top right corner. Select "Report User" and provide as much detail as possible about the incident. Our team will review it shortly.
What happens if an item I receive is broken? expand_more
Since all items are donations, we recommend checking the item at the exchange point. If you find it's not as described after the fact, please leave honest feedback for the donor and report the listing if it was intentionally misleading.
Can I charge a small fee for high-value items? expand_more
No. ReValue Hub is strictly for free exchanges. Attempting to charge money or asking for "tips" is a violation of our terms and will lead to account restriction.
